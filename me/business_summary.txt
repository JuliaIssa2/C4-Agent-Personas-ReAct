Cedar & Crumb is a fictional Beirut bakery that makes small-batch celebration cakes and pastry boxes inspired by Lebanese flavors. Its mission is to make gatherings feel personal while reducing waste through seasonal menus, careful production planning, and recyclable or reusable packaging.

The bakery offers a signature cake collection, made-to-order celebration cakes, corporate pastry boxes, and small weekend baking workshops. Signature cakes need at least 48 hours' notice and start at USD 32. Custom cakes need at least five business days and are quoted after a short consultation. Corporate orders start at 12 boxes and need at least 72 hours' notice. Delivery is available in Beirut and nearby Metn from Tuesday to Saturday, while pickup is available from the fictional Mar Mikhael storefront.

Cedar & Crumb was founded by pastry chef Lina Haddad and operations lead Karim Nassar. Lina develops the seasonal menu and leads custom design consultations; Karim manages sourcing, delivery, and corporate orders. Their unique value proposition is a focused menu that combines familiar celebration formats with local flavors such as orange blossom, pistachio, tahini, carob, and dark chocolate, supported by one clear point of contact from inquiry to pickup or delivery.

The bakery is open Tuesday through Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00, and is closed Sunday and Monday. The kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, sesame, peanuts, and tree nuts, so it cannot guarantee an allergen-free product. Final availability, custom pricing, and delivery fees must be confirmed by the team. General inquiries can be sent to hello@cedarcrumb.example.
